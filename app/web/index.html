    How it works

    Four steps, no guesswork.

    1. You upload a document

      The text is read page by page, so every answer can point back to its page.

    2. It's split into passages

      Long documents become short, overlapping passages that are easy to search.

    3. Your question is matched

      Your question is compared with every passage, and the closest ones are picked.

    4. The answer comes only from them

      The answer is taken from those passages, with the page and passage shown so you can check it.

    Why it sometimes says "Not in this document"

    Many AI tools invent a confident answer when they don't know. This one checks first: if no passage is close enough to your question, or the closest passages don't actually contain the answer, it tells you honestly instead of guessing.
